---
title: Activate Session
description: Set a saved slot as your active profile for the next channel you create.
icon: Play
---

Use `/session activate [slot]` to mark a saved slot as active. The next time you join a generator channel, your saved configuration will be applied automatically.

<Callout title="Note" type="info">
    Slots above your server's slot limit can't be activated. You can still rename or delete them.
</Callout>

Some Creator Channels only load profiles for members with certain roles, or skip parts of a profile. See [Profiles](/configure-channel/others/profiles).
