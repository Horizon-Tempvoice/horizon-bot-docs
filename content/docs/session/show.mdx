---
title: Show Sessions
description: View all your saved session slots.
icon: Eye
---

Use `/session show` to display an overview of all your saved slots, including name, user limit, bitrate, lock/hide state, moderators, trusted and blocked users.

The active slot is marked with a ✦.

Parts your server doesn't store are shown as *not saved on this server*. Slots above the server's slot limit are marked as unavailable.
