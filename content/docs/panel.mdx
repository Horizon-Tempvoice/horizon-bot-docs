Once a temporary channel has been created, ownership is automatically assigned to you, giving you full control over its properties and user management. All of this is handled through a simple interface where everything can be done with just a few clicks.

<ImageWithCaption src="/img/content/horizon_panel.png" alt="Horizon-Panel" caption="The Interface of Horizon" center maxWidth="600px" />

<Callout title="Note" type="info">
    Server admins choose which buttons the panel shows and where it is posted. See [Panel Configuration](/configure-channel/server_settings#panel-configuration).
</Callout>
