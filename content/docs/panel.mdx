---
title: Horizon-Panel
description: Buttons in Discord to manage your temporary voice channel.
icon: MirrorRectangular
---

When Horizon creates a temporary channel for you, you own it. The panel's buttons let you rename it, set a limit, lock or hide it and decide who may join, without typing a command.

<PanelMock />

<Callout title="Note" type="info">
    Server admins choose which buttons the panel shows and where it is posted. See [Panel Configuration](/configure-channel/server/panel_configuration).
</Callout>
