## What is a temporary voice channel?

A voice channel that Horizon creates the moment you join the [creator channel](/configure-channel/generator), and deletes again once everyone has left.

![Join the creator channel, own your channel, and it is gone once everyone has left](/img/welcome/usage.png)

### Why use them

* Channels only exist while someone is in them, so your channel list doesn't fill up with empty rooms.
* The member who created a channel owns it. They can rename it, lock it, set a user limit and more with [commands](/control-channel) or the [Horizon-Panel](/panel), without asking an admin.
* Server admins decide what owners are allowed to change.
