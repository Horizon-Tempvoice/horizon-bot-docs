When Horizon creates a temporary channel for you, you own it. The interface's buttons let you rename it, set a limit, lock or hide it and decide who may join, without typing a command.

<PanelMock />

Renaming your channel, for example, takes three steps:

![Click Rename, type a new name, and your channel is renamed](/img/welcome/panel.png)

<Callout title="Note" type="info">
    Server admins choose which buttons the interface shows and where it is posted. See [Interface Configuration](/configure-channel/server/interface_configuration).
</Callout>
