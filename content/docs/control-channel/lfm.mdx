---
title: Looking for Members
description: Post an announcement so other members can find your channel.
icon: Megaphone
---

Use `/lfm` to post an announcement for your channel in a text channel of your server. A form opens where you describe what you are looking for, between 15 and 200 characters.

The post shows your name, your text and a link to your channel. It is deleted automatically once your channel is gone.

<Callout title="Note" type="info">
    Only the channel owner can post. You can post again every 10 minutes, and a new post replaces the previous one for your channel.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Not available?" type="warn">
    A server admin has to pick the target channel first. See [Looking for Members](/configure-channel/others/lfm_channel).
</Callout>

<Callout title="Vote required" type="info">
    This command needs a [vote](/other-commands#vote) for Horizon within the last 12 hours. Servers with Extended Access don't need this.
</Callout>
