title: Hide / Show Channel
description: Hide your channel from everyone except trusted members, and make it visible again.
icon: EyeOff
---

## Hide

Use `/hide` to make your channel invisible. Only users you have granted access with [`/trust`](/control-channel/trust) can still see and join it.

## Show

Use `/show` to make your channel visible to everyone again after it was hidden.

<Callout title="Channel Moderators" type="info">
    Channel moderators can use these commands if explicitly enabled by the server admin in the dashboard.
</Callout>
