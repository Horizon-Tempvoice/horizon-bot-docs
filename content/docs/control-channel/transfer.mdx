---
title: Transfer Channel
description: Hand over ownership of your temporary channel to another member.
icon: Repeat2
---


Use `/transfer @user` to make another member the new owner of your channel. The target must currently be in the channel. After the transfer, you lose owner privileges.

<Callout title="Note" type="info">
    You cannot transfer to a bot or to yourself.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Vote required" type="info">
    This command needs a [vote](/other-commands#vote) for Horizon within the last 12 hours. Servers with Extended Access don't need this.
</Callout>
