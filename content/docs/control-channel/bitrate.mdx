---
title: Channel Bitrate
description: Adjust the audio bitrate of your temporary channel.
icon: Activity
---

Use `/bitrate` to set the audio quality of your channel. The value is specified in kbps and must be between ``8`` and your server's maximum bitrate (up to ``384`` kbps).

<Callout title="Note" type="idea">
    If you save the channel to a [session](/session), the saved bitrate replaces the default bitrate from the [Dashboard](https://dashboard.horizon-bot.me) whenever that session is loaded.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Channel Moderators" type="info">
    Channel moderators can also use this command. Server admins can disable this in the dashboard.
</Callout>
