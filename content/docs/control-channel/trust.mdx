## Trust

Use `/trust @user` to give a specific member access to your channel even if it is locked or hidden. Trusted users can see and join your channel regardless of its visibility or lock state.

## Untrust

Use `/untrust @user` to revoke the special access you previously granted with `/trust`.

<Callout title="Channel Moderators" type="info">
    Channel moderators can also use these commands. Server admins can disable this in the dashboard.
</Callout>
