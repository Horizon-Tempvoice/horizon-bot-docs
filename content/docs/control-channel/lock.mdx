---
title: Lock / Unlock Channel
description: Stop new users from joining your temporary channel, and open it up again.
icon: Lock
---

## Lock

Use `/lock` to close your channel to new members. Users already inside can stay, but nobody new can join until you unlock it.

## Unlock

Use `/unlock` to open your channel back up after it was locked.

<Callout title="Channel Moderators" type="info">
    Channel moderators can use these commands if explicitly enabled by the server admin in the dashboard.
</Callout>
