Session profiles let you save your current channel configuration, including name, user limit, lock/hide state, trusted users, blocked users, and moderators, and automatically apply it the next time you create a channel.

You can have up to **3 saved slots**, each with an optional label. Your server may allow fewer.

<Callout title="Note" type="info">
    Server admins can disable the session feature. If the commands on this page don't work, sessions may not be available on your server.

    Admins can also choose which parts of a profile are saved and where profiles are loaded. See [Saved Profiles](/configure-channel/generator/saved_profiles).
</Callout>

## Save

Use `/session save [slot] [label]` to save your current channel state. This includes name, user limit, lock/hide state, trusted users, blocked users, and moderators.

If your server doesn't store some of these, the reply lists them.

The optional `label` helps you identify the slot (max. 32 characters).

If you save to the slot your channel was loaded from, only what you changed in this channel is written. Trusted users or blocks that weren't loaded (for example because the generator has a limit) and parts this generator doesn't load stay in the profile.

If you save to any other slot, the channel is copied as it is now and overwrites that slot. Autosave then updates this slot instead.

If your channel is still loading your profile, wait a few seconds before saving.

## Activate

Use `/session activate [slot]` to mark a saved slot as active. The next time you join a generator channel, your saved configuration will be applied automatically.

<Callout title="Note" type="info">
    Slots above your server's slot limit can't be activated. You can still rename or delete them.
</Callout>

Some generators only load profiles for members with certain roles, or skip parts of a profile. See [Saved Profiles](/configure-channel/generator/saved_profiles).

## Skip

Use `/session skip active:True` to skip your active session for the next channel creation. Your active profile stays set, but the next channel will be created without it, as if no session was active.

Set `permanent:True` to keep skipping until you turn it off again. Use `/session skip active:False` to stop skipping.

<Callout title="Note" type="info">
    A one-time skip expires after 2 hours if you don't create a channel in that time. `/session show` tells you whether a skip is active.
</Callout>

## Show

Use `/session show` to display an overview of all your saved slots, including name, user limit, lock/hide state, moderators, trusted and blocked users. For your active slot it also shows whether a skip is active.

The active slot is marked with a ✦.

Parts your server doesn't store are shown as *not saved on this server*. Slots above the server's slot limit are marked as unavailable.

## Rename

Use `/session rename [slot] [label]` to update the label of a saved slot. Leave `label` empty to remove it.

## Delete

Use `/session delete [slot]` to permanently remove a saved session slot.

## Autosave

Use `/session autosave [enabled]` to turn autosave on or off. When enabled, changes you make to your channel are saved to the slot it was loaded from, right when you make them.

- Only what you change is saved. Parts you didn't touch stay as they are in your profile, including trusted users or blocks the channel didn't load.
- Changes made directly in Discord count too, for example renaming the channel or changing its user limit in the channel settings.
- Trusted users who leave the server stay in your profile and are trusted again in your next channel once they're back.

<Callout title="Note" type="info">
    If you don't have any saved slots yet, autosave creates slot 1 for you. If you already have slots, pick one with `/session activate` first.
</Callout>

Server admins can turn autosave on by default. If you set it yourself with `/session autosave`, your choice always wins.

<Callout title="When autosave is skipped" type="warn">
    Your channel state will **not** be saved automatically if:
    - The channel was created with [`/session skip`](#skip), so your profile wasn't loaded
    - The generator only loads profiles for certain roles and you don't have one of them
    - You have saved slots, but none of them is active
    - The server admin has disabled the session feature
    - The channel was transferred or claimed, by you or from you
    - The slot is above the server's slot limit
    - Changes were made while you weren't in the channel

    In all of these cases your profile stays untouched. You can still save manually with [`/session save`](#save).
</Callout>
