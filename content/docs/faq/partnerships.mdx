---
title: Do you accept partnerships?
description: Horizon doesn't take part in partnerships.
icon: Handshake
---

We've been asked about partnerships quite a few times, so here's the answer for everyone: Horizon doesn't partner with servers, bots or other projects. Partnership requests are declined, no matter who sends them.

## What this covers

- Listing each other in partner or affiliate channels
- Shoutouts, cross-promotion or ad swaps
- Deals for special features or treatment in exchange for promotion

## Every server is treated the same

Horizon works the same on a small friend group as on a large community. Size and activity don't unlock anything, and there's no deal that puts a server ahead of others. Promotion wouldn't change anything for the bot, so we keep our time for developing Horizon instead.

<Callout title="Questions about Horizon?" type="info">
    For help with setup, bugs or feature ideas, join our [Support Server](https://support.horizon-bot.me/). Please don't use it to send partnership requests.
</Callout>
