---
title: "{RANDOM_WORD}"
description: Inserts a randomly selected word from a predefined list into your channel name.
---

Each time a channel is created, `{RANDOM_WORD}` picks one word at random from a list you define and inserts it into the channel name.

With the words ``Warrior``, ``Mage``, and ``Rogue`` configured, a template like `` ⚔️・{RANDOM_WORD} `` could produce:

- ``⚔️・Warrior``
- ``⚔️・Mage``
- ``⚔️・Rogue``

<Callout title="Customize the word list" type="idea">
    The field **`{RANDOM_WORD}` Word List** sits right below the name template in the [Channel name](/configure-channel/generator/channel_name) section. Up to 50 words.
</Callout>
