---
title: "{NUMBER}"
description: Numbers the channels of a generator, so they show up as Gaming 1, Gaming 2, Gaming 3.
---

Each new channel gets the lowest number that none of the generator's open channels is using yet. With the template `` 🔊・Gaming {NUMBER} ``, three channels in a row give:

- ``🔊・Gaming 1``
- ``🔊・Gaming 2``
- ``🔊・Gaming 3``

If ``Gaming 2`` is deleted, the next new channel becomes ``Gaming 2`` again.

A channel keeps its number for its whole life. Renaming it with `/name` (when the template also contains [`{CUSTOM}`](/configure-channel/placeholder/CUSTOM)) or handing it to a new owner doesn't change it.

<Callout title="Made for unmanaged generators" type="idea">
    [Unmanaged](/configure-channel/generator/owner_tools#managed-or-unmanaged) channels have no owner, so owner placeholders don't fit them. `{NUMBER}` gives public lobbies a clean name like ``🔊・Gaming 1``, ``🔊・Gaming 2``.
</Callout>

<Callout title="No renumbering" type="info">
    Open channels are never renumbered. If ``Gaming 2`` is deleted while ``Gaming 1`` and ``Gaming 3`` are still open, they stay as they are, and the gap is only filled by the next new channel.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Good to know" type="warn">
    Channels that were already open before you added `{NUMBER}` to the template don't get a number. A channel renamed to a completely different name keeps its number in the background but no longer shows it.
</Callout>
