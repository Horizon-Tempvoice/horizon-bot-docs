<Callout title="Where to find this?" type="info">
    Open the [Dashboard](https://dashboard.horizon-bot.me), pick your server and open **Panel**.
</Callout>

## Panel Buttons

Choose which buttons the [Horizon Panel](/panel) shows and in which order, up to 15 at a time. The preview next to the tiles shows the panel as it will look, in your server's bot language.

- **Reorder:** drag a tile to a new spot.
- **Hide or show:** drag a tile between **Shown on the panel** and **Hidden**, or click it.
- **Full panel:** with all 15 slots in use, drag a hidden tile onto a shown one to swap them.
- **Keyboard:** focus a tile and use the arrow keys to move it; Enter or Space hides or shows it.

**Reset to default** brings back the standard set. Changes are saved with the bar at the bottom of the page.

<Callout title="Note" type="info">
    Buttons for features you turned off in [Owner Tools](/configure-channel/generator/owner_tools) don't work, so it's worth hiding them here too.
</Callout>

## Send Panel

Posts the panel into a text channel of your choice. Save your button changes first, so the panel you send matches the preview. Channels where Horizon is missing a permission are greyed out in the picker, with the missing permission listed.

You can also post it from Discord with `/panel send [channel]`.
