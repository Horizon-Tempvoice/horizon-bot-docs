## Member Log

Posts a short line into the temporary channel's own chat whenever something happens to its members. Everyone in the channel can see at a glance who came, who left and who owns the channel now.

Turn **Member Log** on, then pick which events are posted. At least one event stays on while the member log is enabled.

| Event | Posted when | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Joined** | Someone joins the channel | Alex (alex.dev) `123456789012345678` |
| **Left** | Someone leaves the channel | Alex (alex.dev) `123456789012345678` |
| **Claimed** | Someone takes over the channel with [`/claim`](/control-channel/claim) or the panel | Sam (sam_) `234567890123456789` claimed the channel (previously: Alex (alex.dev) `123456789012345678`) |
| **Transferred** | The owner hands the channel to someone else with [`/transfer`](/control-channel/transfer) or the panel | Alex (alex.dev) `123456789012345678` transferred ownership to Sam (sam_) `234567890123456789` |

Each line starts with a small icon for its event and shows the display name, the username and the user ID, so you can tell people apart even if they share a name. Nobody gets pinged.

<Callout title="Note" type="info">
    Horizon needs **Send Messages** in the temporary channel to post the log. If it can't send there, the line is skipped.
</Callout>

The member log is only a quick overview for the people in the channel. For a permanent record that admins can check later, use the moderation log below.

## Moderation Log

The moderation log records all significant actions in temporary channels created by this generator. Each event is posted as a formatted message to a channel or webhook of your choice.

### What gets logged

<Accordions type="multiple">
    <Accordion title="Channel">
        - Channel created and deleted
        - Channel renamed
        - User limit changed
        - Bitrate changed
        - Region changed
        - Channel claimed or ownership transferred
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Visibility and access">
        - Channel locked or unlocked
        - Channel hidden or made visible again
        - User trusted or untrusted
        - User blocked or unblocked
        - Possible block bypass, if enabled in [Server Settings](/configure-channel/server/settings#blocks)
        - Knock sent, accepted or denied
        - User kicked
        - Invite created
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Moderation">
        - Channel Moderator role added or removed from a user
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Sessions">
        - Session saved, activated or deleted
        - Autosave toggled
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Other">
        - Channel status set or removed
        - Looking for members post created
    </Accordion>
</Accordions>

### Setup

You can send logs to a text channel or a Discord webhook. The box above the switch shows where logs go right now, with **Remove** to stop logging.

Pick **Channel** to let Horizon create a webhook in a channel of your choice. Once the webhook is set up, the mode switches to **Webhook** on its own. This is expected.

Pick **Webhook** and paste your own URL to use an existing webhook.

<Callout title="Saved right away" type="info">
    **Set and save** applies the target immediately. It isn't part of the unsaved-changes bar at the bottom of the page.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Note" type="info">
    If the webhook becomes invalid, Horizon removes it automatically to avoid errors.
</Callout>
