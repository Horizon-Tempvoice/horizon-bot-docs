---
title: Looking for Members
description: Pick the channel where LFM announcements are posted.
---

Pick a text channel and your members can advertise their temporary channel there with [`/lfm`](/control-channel/lfm). Without a channel the command tells them that LFM isn't configured.

<Callout title="Where to find this?" type="info">
    Open the [Dashboard](https://dashboard.horizon-bot.me), pick your server, open **Generators** and choose the generator. This is the **Looking for members** section of its page.
</Callout>

Horizon posts one message per channel, with the owner's name, their text and a link to the voice channel. A new post replaces the previous one of that channel, and the post is deleted once the channel is gone.

Pick the channel and press **Set and save**. Like the mod log, this applies immediately and isn't part of the unsaved-changes bar. Channels where Horizon is missing a permission are greyed out in the picker, with the missing permission listed.

<Callout title="Note" type="info">
    Horizon needs **Send Messages** and **Embed Links** in that channel. Owners can post again every 10 minutes.
</Callout>
