---
title: Immune Roles
description: Let your staff into every temporary channel.
---

Members with an immune role are exempt from what channel owners do. They can't be kicked or blocked, and locks and hides don't keep them out.

<Callout title="Where to find this?" type="info">
    Open the [Dashboard](https://dashboard.horizon-bot.me), select your server, pick the correct Creator Channel and navigate to the **Access Tab**.
</Callout>

| Setting | Description |
|---|---|
| **Immune Roles** | The roles that are exempt. |
| **Allow immune roles to claim** | Lets them use [`/claim`](/control-channel/claim) even while the owner is still in the channel. Off by default. |
| **Apply permission override to immune roles** | On by default: the roles get view and connect on every temporary channel. Turn it off to let them keep exactly the permissions they inherit from the category or Creator Channel, for example a team role that may rename channels. |

<Callout title="Note" type="idea">
    Immunity is meant for moderators. Everyone with the role can enter every channel, including private ones.
</Callout>
