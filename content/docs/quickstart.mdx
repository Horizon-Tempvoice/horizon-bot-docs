## Key concepts

### Generator Channel
The Join to Create channel is a Generator Channel. Horizon creates a temporary channel for every member who joins it. A server can have several Generator Channels, and each one has its own settings for the channels it creates.

<Callout title="Notice" type="warn">
    A server can have up to 3 Generator Channels. There's no limit on the temporary channels they create. Deleting a generator also deletes its settings.
</Callout>

### Tempvoice Panel
The Tempvoice Panel, also called the <strong className="text-fd-primary">Horizon-Panel</strong>, is a Discord message with buttons. Channel owners use it to manage their channel instead of typing slash commands.
