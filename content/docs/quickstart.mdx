## 1. Invite Horizon to your server

Open <a href={INVITE_URL} target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" className="inline-flex items-center gap-1">invite.horizon-bot.me <ExternalLink size={14} /></a> and add <strong className="text-fd-primary">Horizon</strong> to your server.

<div className="mt-2 mb-8">
  <DiscordButton label="Invite Horizon to your Server" href={INVITE_URL} variant="cta" />
</div>

## 2. Set up Horizon in your server

Run `/setup` and click **Setup Horizon**. Horizon creates a <strong className="text-fd-primary">Horizon Channels</strong> category with a <strong className="text-fd-primary">➕ Create your channel</strong> voice channel and an <strong className="text-fd-primary">#interface</strong> channel with the [Horizon-Panel](/panel).

Right after the invite, Horizon also posts a welcome message in your server. Its **Setup Horizon** button does the same as the one in `/setup`.

![Run /setup, click Setup Horizon, and your creator channel is ready](/img/welcome/setup.png)

**Add Generator** and **Add Interface** in the `/setup` menu stay greyed out until your server has its first creator channel. After that, run `/setup` again to add another creator channel or to post the panel in the current channel.

Join <strong className="text-fd-primary">➕ Create your channel</strong> and Horizon creates your own channel and moves you into it.

![Join the creator channel, own your channel, and it is gone once everyone has left](/img/welcome/usage.png)

Everything else is set up in the <a href="https://dashboard.horizon-bot.me" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" className="inline-flex items-center gap-1">Dashboard <ExternalLink size={14} /></a>.

## Key concepts

### Generator Channel
**➕ Create your channel** is a Generator Channel. Horizon creates a temporary channel for every member who joins it. A server can have several Generator Channels, and each one has its own settings for the channels it creates.

<Callout title="Notice" type="warn">
    A server can have up to 3 Generator Channels. There's no limit on the temporary channels they create. Deleting a generator also deletes its settings.
</Callout>

### Horizon-Panel
The <strong className="text-fd-primary">Horizon-Panel</strong> is a Discord message with buttons. Channel owners use it to manage their channel instead of typing slash commands. **Setup Horizon** posts it in a channel called <strong className="text-fd-primary">#interface</strong>.
